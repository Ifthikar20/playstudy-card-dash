A connected parent or guardian can see how the learner is getting on: the learner's name and birth year (if we have it), time studied, XP, streaks, accuracy, how many questions they've answered, how many study sessions they have, which days they studied (with how long they studied and how many questions they answered on each day), and the titles, subjects, accuracy and progress of up to 10 recent study sessions, with when each was started and finished. For a child profile they created, a parent or guardian can also:

For a learner who connected them with a code, a parent or guardian sees progress only and can't change anything. The account stays the learner's own.

AnotherNote doesn't show a parent or guardian written notes, sticky notes or conversations with the AI. There are two exceptions for a child profile they created:

A connection that a learner made by sharing a code ends automatically in the year they turn 18. A parent or guardian can also stop following that learner at any time, and the learner's account carries on as normal.

A child profile that a parent or guardian created doesn't end this way. Until the learner turns 18, it stays with the parent or guardian who created it: it can't be handed over or left without one, so the only way to end it is to delete it. Deleting a child profile closes it straight away. The child is signed out and can't sign in again, and the profile disappears from your Family page. Its study history isn't erased at that moment. It stays on our systems, where nobody can see it in AnotherNote, and we erase it within 60 days of the deletion. If you deleted a profile by mistake, email us within 30 days and we may be able to restore it. To have it erased sooner, email hello@anothernote.app, and we'll erase it and confirm when it's done.

If you delete your own account, we'll also delete the child profiles you created, because nobody else can look after them. Before you do, you can ask us for a copy of what's in them. A connection you made with a learner's code simply ends, and their own account isn't affected.

7AI-generated content

AnotherNote uses artificial intelligence (AI) to split your material into sections and to create notes, quizzes, flashcards, answers, spoken explanations, whiteboard diagrams and short facts from your content and questions. AI also rewrites notes when you use “Ask AI to change”, reviews notes when you use “Check my notes”, and chooses what pictures to look for in Teach mode. To do this, we send the material needed to third-party service providers, including AI model providers and text-to-speech and voice providers (see our Privacy Policy).

AI can be wrong

Outputs can be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date or misleading, even when they sound confident. A quiz can mark a right answer as wrong, and a summary can miss what matters. “Check my notes” can flag something that's actually right, and a suggested fix, or a rewrite from “Ask AI to change”, can be wrong, so only accept a change if you're sure it's correct. Check anything important against your original material, your teachers' guidance or other reliable sources, especially before an exam.

For learning, not grading

AnotherNote's quizzes, scores and accuracy figures are there to help you learn. They aren't designed or tested for grading, exams, admissions, placement or any other formal decision about a learner. Schools and organisations must not use them that way. Parents and guardians can use them to see how a learner is getting on and where they might need help, but shouldn't treat them as a formal assessment.

Not professional advice

Outputs are for learning only. They aren't medical, legal, financial or other professional advice, and you shouldn't rely on them for decisions about your health, safety, money or legal position.

If you need help

AnotherNote is for studying. It isn't a counselling, medical or emergency service, and the AI tutor can't help in a crisis. If you're worried about your safety or someone else's, talk to a trusted adult or contact your local emergency services straight away. In the UK you can call Childline free on 0800 1111. Teach mode and voice features

  • In Teach mode, a synthetic (computer-generated) voice talks you through a section. It doesn't read your notes word for word. AI writes the explanation, which can include examples, formulas on a whiteboard, simple diagrams and pictures, and a pointer shows the line it's talking about. The voice isn't a real person and may mispronounce words. Like any other output, the explanation, diagrams and pictures can be wrong, so check them against your notes and original material.
  • You can ask questions, or dictate notes, by typing or, if you choose, by speaking. Speaking needs your permission to use your microphone.   • Answers are spoken and shown on screen. If your notes don't already cover the answer, AnotherNote may add a short note to the end of that section of its notes. It never adds one to a note you wrote yourself, or to a PDF. Answers can include general knowledge that isn't in your material. Check them as you would any other output.
